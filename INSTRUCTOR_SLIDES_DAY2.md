```

```

```

```

**Answer Key for Instructor:**

**Prompt 1 Missing:**
- Which aspects of paper? (Methods? Results? All sections?)
- For whom? (Experts vs general audience?)
- What depth? (Tweet vs detailed summary?)
- What format? (Bullets, prose, table?)
- Why summarising? (Affects focus)

**Prompt 2 Missing:**
- What type of data? (SEM images? Stress-strain curves? Spectroscopy?)
- What analysis needed? (Statistical? Comparative? Qualitative?)
- What output format? (Table? Graph? Report?)
- What to do with outliers/missing data?
- Success criteria?

**Prompt 3 Missing:**
- Which material system?
- What specific help? (Troubleshooting? New protocol? Optimisation?)
- What constraints? (Equipment, safety, budget?)
- Current situation/problem?
- What have you tried?

## SLIDE 11: The Five W's + H Framework

**Visual:**
- Six question boxes arranged in circle
- Each with icon and example

**Content:**
```
Context Building: The Five W's + H

Who?   → Who is this for? Who are you?
What?  → What specific task needs doing?
When?  → What timeframe/deadline/era?
Where? → What domain/location/context?
Why?   → What's the purpose/goal?
How?   → How should it be done?

Use this checklist for every prompt
```

**Design Notes:**
- Circular arrangement of 6 boxes
- Icons for each W/H
- Red connecting lines showing relationship
- Checklist format

**Speaker Notes:**
> Simple framework for building context: Five W's plus H. Who is this for? What task specifically? When (timeframe)? Where (domain context)? Why (purpose)? How (method)? Run through this mental checklist before every prompt. It takes 30 seconds and saves 10 minutes of iteration.

(3 months, 5 users) rather than wholesale rejection or indefinite 
"we'll think about it."

using quantitative data where possible. Acknowledge risks honestly 
rather than overselling. Think "credible colleague recommendation" 
not "sales pitch." Use clear structure with bullet points for 
scannability.

## APPENDIX: Facilitation Notes for Day 2

### Timing Guidance (Total: ~90 minutes)
- Welcome & Day 1 Recap: 5-10 min
- Context Deep Dive: 25-30 min (including activities)
- CO-STAR Framework: 30-35 min (including activities)
- Hallucination Hunt: 25-30 min (competitive tournament)
- Wrap-up & Reflection: 5-10 min

### Interactive Elements Summary (Total: 19 activities)
- **Live Demos:** 5 (context refinement, CO-STAR building)
- **Small Group Activities:** 3 (context diagnosis, CO-STAR building, hallucination hunt)
- **Pair Work:** 2 (context refinement practice)
- **Think-Pair-Share:** 3 (quick collaborative discussions)
- **Polls/Quick Checks:** 3 (framework selection, anti-patterns)
- **Volunteer Share-Outs:** 3 (group presentations)

### Materials Needed
- Laptop with sandbox running
- Projector for slides + screen share
- **Whiteboard/flipchart with markers** for leaderboard tracking
  - Format: Team Name | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 | Speed Bonus | Total
  - Alternative: Projected Excel/Google Sheet visible to all
- Printed handouts (optional but recommended):
  - Failed prompts for Activity 1
  - CO-STAR scenario for Activity 2
  - Hallucination Hunt reports (Rounds 1 & 2) for tournament
- **Timer (visible to all)** - countdown clock on screen or physical timer
- Prizes/badges for tournament winners (optional but recommended)
  - "Eagle Eye" certificates or small prizes

### Group Formation Tips
- Pre-assign groups if you know skill levels (mix abilities)
- Or: Let groups self-form naturally (faster)
- Ensure groups of 3-4 (not pairs for group activities)
- Rotate group members between activities to build team connections
- Have a plan for odd numbers (one group of 5 is okay)

### Tournament Facilitation Tips
- **Energy is key:** Keep excitement high, celebrate catches
- **Fairness:** If dispute on error identification, discuss openly
- **Scoring:** Use visible leaderboard (whiteboard or projected)
- **Hints:** If all teams stuck, give subtle hints to keep momentum
- **Time management:** Stick to timers but be slightly flexible if needed
- **Prizes:** Even small recognition (verbal praise, certificates) motivates

### Common Challenges & Solutions

**Challenge:** Activities run over time
- **Solution:** Build 5-min buffer between sections, cut reflection time if needed

**Challenge:** One group dominates discussions
- **Solution:** Call on quieter groups first, rotate who presents

**Challenge:** Sandbox slow with multiple users
- **Solution:** Pre-load model, limit simultaneous queries, use demo mode if needed

**Challenge:** Participants don't understand CO-STAR vs AUTOMAT
- **Solution:** Use decision tree repeatedly, give more examples, have them explain back

**Challenge:** Hallucination hunt too easy/hard
- **Solution:** Have backup errors ready (easier or harder), adjust Round 3 based on Round 1-2 performance

### Adaptation Notes
- **If running ahead:** Add more examples, deeper discussions, bonus round to tournament
- **If running behind:** Skip Slide 39 (parking lot), shorten reflection, reduce tournament to 2 rounds
- **For remote delivery:** Use breakout rooms for group work, digital scoring tools, screen share heavily
- **For large groups (>20):** Pre-assign groups, use polling tools, limit share-outs to 1-2 groups

### Energy Management
- **High energy:** Tournament, live demos, competitive elements
- **Medium energy:** Group activities, discussions
- **Lower energy:** Instruction slides, examples
- **Pattern:** Alternate high/medium/lower throughout to maintain engagement

### Follow-Up Actions
- Update FAQ based on parking lot questions
- Email Day 2 summary with highlights
- Share group photo
- Provide Day 3 pre-reading links
- Individual follow-up if someone struggled
